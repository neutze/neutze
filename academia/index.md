# Academia

My B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Informatics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), 2012–2016:
two theses, a conference paper and an interdisciplinary project.

---

## Master's thesis, 2016

**Analysis of Android Cracking Tools and Investigations into Countermeasures for Developers**

TUM, Division for Operating Systems · Supervisor Prof. Dr. Uwe Baumgarten · Advisor Nils Kannengießer, M.Sc.

A black-box analysis of Lucky Patcher, one of the major tools used to attack license
verification on Android. Its strategy is traced back to the single concept of a unary decision,
and countermeasures are proposed, from fortifying the current approach to disruptive change,
each weighed between security, openness and complexity.

**[Read the thesis (PDF, 2.1 MB)](2016-master-thesis-android-cracking-tools.pdf)**

## Conference paper, 2016

**An Insight to Cracking Solutions and Circumvention of Major Protection Methods for Android**

15th AmiEs, Heraklion, Crete, Greece · N. Kannengießer, J. Neutze, U. Baumgarten, S. Song

Android is a target of software piracy. Application stores counteract it with license
verification libraries, but even those do not stop theft of intellectual property. The paper
presents the attack methods and outlines countermeasures.

## Bachelor's thesis, 2013

**Implementation of a HomeMatic Simulator Using Android**

TUM · Supervisor Prof. Dr. Uwe Baumgarten · Advisor Nils Kannengießer, M.Sc.

An Android application that simulates the HomeMatic home-automation system and its XML-API v1.2,
so clients such as HomeDroid can connect and interact with it like with the real hardware.
